About

Good design and user experience matter.

I've spent the last seven years building production software. At Nikola Motor Company I worked on the infotainment and instrument cluster screens commercial truck drivers relied on every day. At SyberJet / Alpine Landing, I was part of the team that built a new, complex web application ahead of launch and laid the foundation for its companion mobile app.

Frontend is where I'm happiest. I care about the details: spacing, states, how an interaction feels. I like working closely with product and design to turn real user needs into software that's simple to use.

Before software, I earned a B.S. in Human Nutrition from Arizona State University and worked in healthcare at Mayo Clinic and Banner Health, which is why health tech and mission-driven products are close to my heart. I got into coding thanks to my sister, a self-taught principal engineer, and I'm working toward senior and lead frontend roles myself.
